Gimbal Protocol · end-to-end map

An ETH-backed soft-peg built on options, not debt. Lock ETH, split it into two fully-collateralized legs — a stable tracking leg and a leveraged-ETH leg. No liquidations, no funding, no debt — ever.
P + N = 1 ETH · always No liquidation · fully collateralized Slow oracle · read once at settlement Network · Sepolia testnet · research code, unaudited

merge() recombines the two legs back into ETH at any time, with no oracle — your exit never depends on a price feed. settle() runs once at maturity: it pulls the index x (ETH priced in the asset) and fixes payout P = min(1, S/x), payout N = 1 − payout P. Conservation is exact — no drift, no bad debt.
